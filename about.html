About the Ten Spiritual Vibrations

Telepathic Race: Secrets of the Ten Spiritual Vibrations explores spiritual awareness, personal development, and the relationship between thoughts, choice, and the human experience.

Understanding the Ten Spiritual Vibrations

The Telepathic Race philosophy presents a sequence of spiritual vibrations that represent a higher understanding of their numerical value and evolved symbolism.

  2. Thought

  4. Love
  5. Revelation
  6. People
  7. Snake
  8. Leadership
  9. Infinity
  10. Death
  11. Perfect

The Telepathic Race Philosophy

Telepathic Race encourages participants to explore the concept of a telepathic connection to an Infinite God through conscience.

Through educational resources and virtual meetings, visitors can explore this and other Telepathic Race concepts, such as underlying hand gesture recognition, as they develop their own understanding of the Ten Spiritual Vibrations.
