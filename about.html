About the Ten Spiritual Vibrations

Telepathic Race: Secrets of the Ten Spiritual Vibrations explores spiritual awareness, personal development, and the relationship between thoughts, emotions, and human experiences.

Understanding the Ten Spiritual Vibrations

The Telepathic Race philosophy presents a sequence of spiritual vibrations that represent different concepts and stages of personal understanding.

  1. Nothing
  2. Thought
  3. Question
  4. Love
  5. Revelation
  6. People
  7. Snake
  8. Leadership
  9. Infinity
  10. Death
  11. Perfect

The Telepathic Race Philosophy

Telepathic Race encourages participants to explore the connections between thought, questioning, love, revelation, and personal growth.

Through educational resources and virtual meetings, visitors can explore these ideas and develop their own understanding of the Ten Spiritual Vibrations.
